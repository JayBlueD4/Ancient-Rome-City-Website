The Roman Colosseum

The Roman Colosseum (right image) is one of the most famous buildings built in the Roman Empire. It served as an amphitheatre for the Roman Empire during the years of Ancient Rome. An amphitheatre is an open-air venue used for entertainment, sports, and performances. In fact, the Colosseum is actually the largest amphitheatre built during ancient times. It is located is the center of Rome, and is still the largest amphitheatre today. During the times of Ancient Rome, the Roman Empire used the Colosseum to host gladiator fights, animal hunts, and more. While it has been damaged from multiple earthquakes, the Roman Colosseum still remains as the iconic symbol of Imperial Rome, and is one of the New 7 Wonders of the World.
